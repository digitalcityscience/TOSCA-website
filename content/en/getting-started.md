# Getting started with TOSCA

TOSCA stands for **Toolkit for Open and Sustainable City Planning and Analysis**. It supports collaborative work with geographic information in urban planning.

## Start with a planning question

Different projects apply TOSCA to different questions: identifying land for affordable housing, preparing evacuation plans, or understanding accessibility. First establish the place, the question, and the people involved.

## Understand your project context

Each deployment may have different datasets, modules, and access arrangements. Ask your project team which application and data are appropriate for your work.

## Explore the project story

The [project directory](/projects) introduces the research and collaborations behind the toolkit. Core TOSCA projects established and extended it; contributing projects informed its development. Current development continues within SUMO_15 / NGCN.

## About this guide

This is an introductory guide. Detailed application walkthroughs will be authored and reviewed alongside the relevant application version. It does not yet document individual controls or claim that every deployment has the same functionality.
